Step 1 of 6
Blackbird Terminal

Guest checks in through the app

When a guest wants to pay with Blackbird, they open the app and check in. Their name and a unique 6-digit code appear instantly on your Blackbird Terminal — like BB4911.

💡
Each code is unique to that visit. It changes every time — always use the one currently on screen.
Step 2 of 6
Toast POS

Type the code into the Tab Name field

On Toast, tap the Tab Name field at the top and type the guest's 6-digit code exactly as shown — BB4911.

⚠️
This is the most critical step. If the code is wrong or missing, Blackbird cannot route the payment and your restaurant won't get paid.
📋
Optional: Add the guest's name with a space before or after — e.g. Ally BB4911
Step 3 of 6
Toast POS

Place the order as usual

Ring in the order on Toast exactly as you normally would. The code you entered is all Blackbird needs — nothing else changes in your workflow.

Step 4 of 6
Toast POS

Select Blackbird Pay as the tender type

When closing the check, tap Blackbird Pay from the tender options — the same way you'd select card or cash. No card swipe needed.

💳
Payment processes automatically through the guest's linked account in the Blackbird app.
Step 5 of 6
Toast POS

Guest selects their tip on Toast

The customer-facing tip screen appears as normal. The guest selects their tip amount just like any other payment — nothing for you to do here.

👤
Entirely guest-driven. Wait for them to confirm their tip, then return to the Blackbird terminal.
Step 6 of 6
Blackbird Terminal

Tap Confirm to close the check

Return to your Blackbird Terminal and tap Confirm under the guest's name. This clears the check from the terminal and fully closes the transaction.

⚠️
Don't skip this step. If you don't tap Confirm, the check stays open on the terminal and the transaction won't fully settle.
🎉
You're done. Payment settled, check cleared, guest earned their $FLY rewards.
