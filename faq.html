Getting Started

Blackbird is the app for restaurant lovers, offering the richest rewards in dining. Earn points every time you dine and pay with Blackbird, which you can use here or at any spot across the network. The more you dine, the more you unlock, including access to Blackbird Club, Blackbird's loyalty program for the restaurant obsessed.

"We accept Blackbird, an app that rewards you with points ($FLY) for dining out. You can pay in the app and use your points like cash here and at other great restaurants in the Blackbird network."

Guests check in by scanning their phone on the Blackbird puck at the host stand (FSR) or register (QSR), or via GPS through the Blackbird app.

After check-in, guests are prompted to confirm if they're paying with Blackbird. If yes, their name appears under Open Tabs on the Terminal. If no, they'll show under All Guests.

The purple puck is for employees — tapping it unlocks Industry Status in the Blackbird app, which gives access to exclusive Blackbird-funded perks and rewards designed for restaurant workers.

Employees must physically tap the purple puck in order to get, and maintain, industry status. (GPS check-ins don't work for this.)

$FLY are the points you earn when you pay with Blackbird. 100 $FLY = $1 in spending power.

Use your $FLY like cash at any restaurant in the network. You don't earn $FLY when paying with $FLY.

100 $FLY = $1

Guests can spend $FLY at any restaurant in the Blackbird network. There are thousands of participating restaurants. To view all locations that accept Blackbird Pay, guests can open the app and tap Map.

Payment Troubleshooting

First, make sure they've added a valid credit/debit card to their account.

Then, ask them to check in again and tap "Yes" when prompted to pay with Blackbird.

Make sure they have toggled on "Pay with $FLY" from their check settings page. If they accidentally charged their card instead, have them contact support@blackbird.xyz for help.

If everyone is paying with Blackbird, have them tap Split the Check — this creates one combined check on the Terminal. If only some are using Blackbird, split the check in your POS. First, close out the non-Blackbird guests, then close out the Blackbird guests.

This feature lets you place a temporary hold on a guest's payment method for the amount of their order mid-service. This ensures the card is valid and funds are available, giving you confidence the payment will clear when the check is closed.

On the Blackbird Terminal:

  • Tap the guest tab
  • Tap Pre-Authorize
  • Enter the amount, usually equal to the amount of the guest's order thus far
  • Blackbird attempts to pre-authorize the payment
  • If pre-authorization fails, collect another form of payment outside of Blackbird Pay
  • Blackbird will not honor any payment after a failed pre-authorization
  • Ask the guest to make sure the app is updated
  • Suggest restarting the app and trying again
  • If issues continue, the guest can contact Blackbird Support through the app or email support@blackbird.xyz
Important: Always collect another form of payment to complete the transaction if the issue isn't resolved quickly.

Auto-gratuity is already added to the check. Remind guests to set their in-app tip to 0% if they don't want to add an additional tip.

Terminal Troubleshooting

Locked out? Your login credentials are auto-saved. Tap Log In, tap into the email field (credentials auto-populate), then Sign In. If credentials don't appear, contact partners@blackbird.xyz or text (646) 798-9362.

1. Confirm power source

  • The Blackbird Terminal must be plugged directly into a wall outlet.
  • Do not plug it into a POS system, POS printer, or shared USB hub.
  • The Blackbird Terminal may be plugged into a Toast printer once the device is at 100% charge.

2. Check charging connections

  • Ensure the charging cord is fully seated in the back of the Blackbird Terminal charging stand.
  • Confirm the Blackbird Terminal is sitting upright and securely in the stand.

1. Check WiFi strength

  • Confirm the WiFi network is working properly (test on another device).
  • If needed, reset the router.
  • The Blackbird Terminal cannot connect to public WiFi networks.

2. Connect to a new WiFi network

  • Swipe up on the Blackbird Terminal screen → Tap the circle at the bottom → Tap the three dots in the top-right corner → Select WiFi and choose the correct network.
  • If you see an "Authentication Failure" message, an incorrect password was entered. Select the network again → Tap Modify Network and re-enter the correct password.

1. Restart the device

  • Hold the power button (on the side of the device) for 10 seconds.
  • Wait for the device to power back on. A black screen often means the battery is drained or the device is powered off.

2. Check charging position

  • Make sure the Blackbird Terminal is seated properly in the charging stand.
  • Confirm the stand is plugged into a wall outlet.

1. Confirm Scalefusion is running (newer devices)

  • If Scalefusion has been exited, the device will show multiple apps instead of the Blackbird screen.
  • Swipe up to access apps, then locate and open the Scalefusion app.

2. For older devices

  • If you see a bird icon, tap it to return to the Blackbird screen.

1. About PIN codes

  • Every employee with a Blackbird account has a unique 4-digit PIN — the last 4 digits of the phone number linked to their Blackbird account.

2. Activating a PIN

  • Tap your phone to the purple employee puck at your restaurant. Make sure you have the Blackbird app downloaded.
  • This action recognizes you as staff and activates your PIN.

3. If a PIN stops working

  • Employees must tap the employee puck at least once every 2 weeks.
  • 2+ weeks of inactivity will clear industry status.
  • If needed, a manager's PIN can be used temporarily.

4. Viewing or editing PINs

  • In Back of House, navigate to: Settings → Staff PIN Management → Tap the three dots → Select Reset/Edit PIN.

PINs ensure accurate attribution and added security when using the Blackbird Terminal — similar to logging into a POS with a PIN. They allow us to:

  • Process charges securely, just like a POS system
  • Provide better visibility into which team member closed which check (helpful when issues arise with incorrectly closed checks)
  • Track individual performance when needed
  • Run staff challenges more efficiently — rewards can now be attributed and distributed automatically, making challenges fairer and easier to manage

Managers can remove former employees in Back of House: Settings → Staff PIN Management → Tap the three dots → Delete staff member.

This immediately prevents them from logging in or closing checks.

For staff challenges and accurate tracking, we recommend the team member handling the table closes the check whenever possible.

However, if managers need to close checks, they can do so using their own PIN.
